Modern Forms Fan

Not ready for use. This build is the first step of a new driver for Modern Forms smart ceiling fans and their lights: it proves that the driver can be built, tested and packaged, and it does not control a fan. Do not add it to a project; keep your fan on the driver it uses today. What the driver will do, and how a fan moves to it from the driver it uses now, is being written down as the driver's first contract; nothing on this page describes it yet.

What this build does

Nothing that reaches a fan. It has no connections, actions, events or programming, and shows no tile on Navigators. Its Properties tab has two rows:

RowWhat it shows
Driver VersionThe driver's build number, for example 12; a newer build has a higher one.
Debug ModeOff (the default) or On. On, the driver writes its debug lines to Composer's Lua output; off, only warnings and errors.

A development build also shows a Dev Server row, for the developer's test setup; release builds do not have it.

Where to follow the work

The project, its plans and its issues are on GitHub: github.com/corytron/c4-modernforms-driver. The changes of every release are listed at the end of this page.
